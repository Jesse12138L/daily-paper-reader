## 摘要
世界-动作模型（WAM）有望成为一个统一模型，能够预测以动作为条件的未来、生成可行动作，并支持在想象中进行规划。然而，现有的联合视频-动作模型通常采用计算开销大、固定时域的主干网络，不适合流式推理和稳定的长时域开环推演。我们提出 FLEX-WAM，一种灵活且高效的块因果世界-动作模型，用于统一仿真与策略推理。FLEX-WAM 支持可变长度上下文和非因果预测时域，也支持逐帧或逐块的无限自回归生成。其块因果、可 KV 缓存的架构结合了轴向注意力与分块扩散强制，无需重新训练即可实现高效实时推演以及部署时延迟-吞吐量权衡。然而，联合训练仍可能产生对指令动作响应较弱的看似合理的未来。我们通过平衡状态-动作扩散噪声网格上的状态与动作流匹配梯度贡献，并利用前向动力学（FD）弹性（一种高效的训练时动作响应性代理）调节世界模型采样，来解决这一失效模式。在仿真和真实世界数据集上，FLEX-WAM 实现了更优的多步预测质量和延迟，同时产生可稳定进行数千步的联合状态-动作推演。作为 MCTS 中的联合动作提议器和模拟器，它完全在想象中求解长时域 PushT 以及全部五个 OGBench Puzzle-4x4 任务。在基于 OpenArm 的双臂机器人上，单个检查点可同时充当执行策略和预期结果预测器，从而实时识别并收集模型-现实不匹配，以用于未来的自我改进。

## Abstract
World--action models (WAMs) promise a unified model that predicts action-conditioned futures, generates feasible actions, and supports planning in imagination. However, existing joint video--action models often use computationally heavy, fixed-horizon backbones ill-suited to streaming inference and stable long-horizon open-loop rollouts. We introduce FLEX-WAM, a Flexible and Efficient Block-Causal World--Action Model for unified simulation and policy inference. FLEX-WAM supports variable-length contexts and non-causal prediction horizons, as well as infinite autoregressive generation frame by frame or block by block. Its block-causal, KV-cacheable architecture combines axial attention and blockwise diffusion forcing to enable efficient real-time rollout and deployment-time latency--throughput tradeoffs without retraining. Joint training can nevertheless produce plausible futures that weakly respond to commanded actions. We address this failure mode by balancing state and action flow-matching gradient contributions across the state--action diffusion-noise grid and regulating world-model sampling using Forward-Dynamics (FD) elasticity, an efficient training-time proxy for action responsiveness. Across simulated and real-world datasets, FLEX-WAM achieves superior multi-step prediction quality and latency while producing stable joint state--action rollouts for thousands of steps. As a joint action proposer and simulator within MCTS, it solves long-horizon PushT and all five OGBench Puzzle-4x4 tasks entirely in imagination. On a bimanual OpenArm-based robot, a single checkpoint jointly serves as a play policy and expected-outcome predictor, enabling real-time identification and collection of model--reality mismatches for future self-improvement.
