## 摘要
世界动作模型（WAM）联合预测动作（意图）与视觉未来（预见）。标准训练会同时向记录的动作和视频添加噪声，但这类训练范式引入了一种不匹配：被扰动的动作意味着反事实的未来视觉，而加噪视频仍与 GT 记录绑定。在低噪声情形下，尽管添加了噪声，场景几何乃至动态行为仍可从加噪的未来帧中清晰看出。我们提出 CtrlWAM，它在模拟器中执行被扰动的动作，并将其与相应的加噪视觉后果配对，用于联合 WAM 学习。为适应视频与动作不同的去噪需求，我们引入扭曲的视频—动作噪声调度，旨在随着动作预测的演化保持视觉布局的响应性。我们进一步将动作接口从仅自我（ego-only）控制扩展到可变数量的智能体流，使统一模型能够表示多个智能体的预测未来或指令未来。驾驶实验表明动作预测更准确、生成视频与动作之间更一致，并且能更好地遵循给定指令；机器人实验表明运动保真度和可控性更强。匹配对照支持了离路径渲染（off-path renders）对指令遵循和操作保真度的益处。综上，这些发现有助于构建更可控的世界动作模型。项目页面：https://ctrl-wam.github.io/

## Abstract
World action models (WAMs) jointly predict actions (intent) and visual future (foresight). Standard training adds noise to recorded actions and video simultaneously, but such training paradigms introduce a mismatch: perturbed actions imply counterfactual future visual, while the noised video remains tied to the GT recording. In low-noise regime, the scene geometry and even the dynamic behavior remain clearly visible from the noisy future frames despite the added noise. We present CtrlWAM, which executes perturbed actions in a simulator and pairs them with their noised visual consequences for joint WAM learning. To accommodate the different denoising requirements of video and actions, we introduce warped video--action noise schedules that aim to keep visual layout responsive as action predictions evolve. We further extend the action interface from ego-only control to a variable number of agent streams, allowing a unified model to represent predicted or commanded futures for multiple agents. Driving experiments show more accurate action forecasts, closer agreement between generated video and actions, and better following of supplied commands; robotics experiments show stronger motion fidelity and controllability. Matched controls support the benefit of off-path renders for command following and manipulation fidelity. Together, these findings contribute to a more controllable world action model.
